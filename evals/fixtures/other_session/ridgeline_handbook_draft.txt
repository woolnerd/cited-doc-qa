Ridgeline Outfitters Employee Handbook (Draft Revision)

Fictional document written for the cited-doc-qa retrieval tests. It is uploaded under a different session, so no question in the eval should ever retrieve it. Its numbers deliberately differ from employee_handbook.txt.

Introductory Period

Every new employee serves a 120-day introductory period, with check-ins at 45 and 90 days.

Paid Time Off

Full-time employees earn 20 days of paid time off per year from their first day. Unused PTO carries over without limit.

Overtime

Hourly employees are paid double time for all hours worked over 45 in a workweek.

Retirement Plan

Ridgeline matches 100 percent of the first 8 percent of pay contributed to the 401(k) plan.

Attendance

Missing one scheduled shift without calling in is treated as a voluntary resignation.

Tuition Assistance

Ridgeline reimburses up to 3,000 dollars per year for approved college courses.
