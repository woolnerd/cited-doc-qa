Copper Kettle Bakery Staff Handbook

Fictional document written for the cited-doc-qa retrieval tests. Copper Kettle Bakery is not a real company.

About This Handbook

Copper Kettle Bakery runs two retail bakeries and a wholesale kitchen that supplies cafes and grocery stores. This handbook explains what we expect of staff and what staff can expect from us. The owners may update it at any time and will post changes in the break room at least two weeks before they take effect.

Who Is Covered

The handbook covers bakers, counter staff, drivers, and office staff. Full-time staff are scheduled for 35 or more hours per week. Part-time staff are scheduled for fewer than 35 hours per week.

Probationary Period

New staff serve a 60-day probationary period. At the end of the probationary period the head baker or shop lead holds a short review with you. Staff who are promoted into a lead role serve a new 30-day probationary period in that role.

Shifts and Scheduling

The wholesale kitchen runs from 3:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. Retail shifts run between 6:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. Schedules are posted two weeks ahead on the board by the walk-in cooler. To swap a shift, write both names on the swap sheet and get the shop lead's initials before the shift starts.

Overtime

Hourly staff are paid one and a half times their regular rate for any hours over 8 in a single workday and for any hours over 40 in a workweek. Hours over 12 in a single workday are paid at double time. Overtime must be approved by the head baker or shop lead before you work it.

Meal Breaks

Staff who work more than 5 hours get an unpaid 30-minute meal break. Staff also get a paid 10-minute rest break for every 4 hours worked. Bakers on the early wholesale shift may take their meal break after the last delivery van is loaded.

Calling Out

If you cannot make a shift, call the shop lead's phone as early as possible and no later than 90 minutes before your shift. Wholesale bakers must call by 1:30 a.m. so the morning bake can be covered. Three no-call no-shows in a rolling 12 months is grounds for termination.

Vacation

Full-time staff earn 12 days of paid vacation per year, accrued each pay period. Vacation may not be taken in the two weeks before Thanksgiving or the two weeks before Christmas, which are our busiest weeks. Unused vacation does not carry over; staff may cash out up to 5 unused days each December.

Sick Time

All staff earn one hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours per year. Food safety rules apply: do not come to work with vomiting, diarrhea, fever, or a sore throat with fever. Stay home until 24 hours after symptoms end, and tell the shop lead why you are out so we can follow the health department's reporting rules.

Holidays

The bakery is closed on New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Full-time staff are paid for six holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Staff who work on Memorial Day, Independence Day, or Labor Day are paid double time for those hours.

Health Benefits

Staff who average 25 or more hours per week can enroll in the bakery's medical and dental plans. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after 90 days of employment. The bakery pays 70 percent of the premium for staff-only coverage and 50 percent for family coverage.

Retirement Savings

Staff may join the Copper Kettle 401(k) plan after six months of employment. The bakery matches 100 percent of the first 3 percent of pay that you contribute. Matching contributions vest immediately.

Staff Discount and Meals

Staff get one free drink and one pastry per shift. Staff pay 50 percent of the retail price for day-old bread and pastries, and 20 percent off fresh retail items. Take-home items must be rung up at the register by the shop lead.

Uniform and Hygiene

Wear the bakery apron, a hat or hair net, and non-slip shoes. Remove rings, watches, and bracelets before handling dough. Wash your hands when you start your shift, after breaks, and after handling raw eggs.

Injuries and Safety

Report every injury, including small burns and cuts, to the shop lead immediately and before the end of your shift. First aid kits and burn gel are by the hand sinks in both kitchens. Only staff trained by the head baker may use the dough sheeter or clean the spiral mixer.

Resigning

Please give at least one week of notice if you resign. Final pay is ready on your last day and includes any unused vacation you have earned.
