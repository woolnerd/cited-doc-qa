Ridgeline Outfitters Employee Handbook

Welcome

Ridgeline Outfitters sells camping, climbing, and paddling gear from nine stores and one distribution center. This handbook applies to every hourly and salaried employee. Where a store manager's instructions conflict with this handbook, the handbook controls. Questions about any policy go to your store manager first and then to People Operations.

Employment Status

Full-time employees are scheduled for 32 or more hours per week. Part-time employees are scheduled for fewer than 32 hours per week. Seasonal employees are hired for a fixed period, usually the summer season or the holiday season, and are told their end date in their offer letter. Seasonal employees who work more than 1,000 hours in a calendar year are reclassified as part-time.

Introductory Period

Every new employee serves a 90-day introductory period. During this period your manager meets with you at 30, 60, and 90 days to review your progress. The introductory period may be extended once, by up to 30 days, if your manager and People Operations agree that more time is needed. Completing the introductory period does not change the at-will nature of employment.

Work Schedules and Timekeeping

Store schedules are posted every Thursday for the week starting the following Monday. Employees clock in and out on the store terminal or the Ridgeline Crew app. Do not clock in more than 7 minutes before a scheduled shift without a manager's approval. Shift trades are allowed when both employees are trained for the role and the trade is approved in the app at least 24 hours before the shift.

Overtime

Hourly employees are paid overtime at one and a half times their regular rate for all hours worked over 40 in a workweek. The workweek runs from Monday at 12:00 a.m. to Sunday at 11:59 p.m. Overtime must be approved in advance by a store manager. Working unapproved overtime is a policy violation, but you will still be paid for every hour you work.

Meal and Rest Breaks

Employees scheduled for more than 6 hours receive an unpaid 30-minute meal break, taken no later than the end of the fifth hour of work. Employees also receive a paid 10-minute rest break for every 4 hours worked. Breaks may not be combined or skipped to leave early.

Attendance

If you will be late or absent, call the store directly at least 2 hours before your shift. A text message to a coworker does not count as notice. Missing two consecutive scheduled shifts without calling in is treated as a voluntary resignation.

Paid Time Off

Full-time employees earn paid time off (PTO) for vacation and personal days. In the first three years of employment, full-time employees earn 10 days of PTO per year. After three years of service, they earn 15 days per year. PTO accrues each pay period and may be used once it has accrued. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1. Part-time employees do not earn PTO but do earn sick leave.

PTO requests for more than three consecutive days must be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Requests during the November and December holiday season are limited to two days per employee.

Sick Leave

All employees earn one hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours per year. Sick leave may be used for your own illness or medical care, or to care for a family member. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than three consecutive scheduled shifts.

Holidays

Stores close on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Full-time employees receive holiday pay for seven holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and one floating holiday chosen each year. Hourly employees who work on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, or Labor Day are paid one and a half times their regular rate for those hours.

Benefits Eligibility

Employees who average 30 or more hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage. Coverage begins on the first day of the month after 60 days of employment. Ridgeline pays 80 percent of the employee-only medical premium.

Retirement Plan

Employees age 21 or older may join the Ridgeline 401(k) plan after 90 days of employment. Ridgeline matches 50 percent of the first 6 percent of pay that you contribute. Matching contributions vest over three years, one third per year of service.

Employee Discount

Employees receive a 25 percent discount on regular-priced merchandise and a 10 percent discount on sale merchandise. The discount covers purchases for yourself and your household only. Gear rentals for employees are free when booked at least two days ahead.

Dress Code

Wear the Ridgeline crew vest and closed-toe shoes on the sales floor. Logos from competing outdoor brands may not be visible while you are on shift. Distribution center employees must also wear steel-toe boots.

Workplace Safety

Report any injury, however small, to the manager on duty within 24 hours. The manager files an incident report the same day. Only employees who have completed forklift certification may operate the forklift or the pallet jack in the distribution center.

Leaving Ridgeline

Please give at least two weeks' written notice if you resign. Your final paycheck includes all hours worked and, for full-time employees, any accrued and unused PTO. Return your crew vest, keys, and badge on your last day.
