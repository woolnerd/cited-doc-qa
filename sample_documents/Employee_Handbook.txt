WELCOME

Welcome to FreeTime AI. This handbook outlines the policies, expectations, and benefits that apply to all employees. Please read it carefully and keep it as a reference throughout your employment.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 1 — CODE OF CONDUCT

1.1 Professional Standards
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, respect, and professionalism in every interaction — with colleagues, customers, and partners. We hold ourselves to a high standard of honest communication and ethical behavior.

1.2 Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company information, including product roadmaps, customer data, financial information, and proprietary technology. This obligation continues after employment ends.

1.3 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, investments, or relationships that could create a conflict of interest with their role at FreeTime AI. Approval from your manager and HR is required before engaging in outside consulting or advisory work.

1.4 Workplace Respect
FreeTime AI is committed to a harassment-free workplace. Discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

SECTION 2 — WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

2.1 Standard Hours
The standard workweek is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in your local time zone, during which all employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration.

2.2 Flexible Work Arrangements
FreeTime AI supports flexible scheduling. Employees may adjust their start and end times outside of core hours with manager approval. Remote work is permitted for all roles unless otherwise specified in your employment agreement.

2.3 Time Off
Employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, increasing to 20 days after three years of service. PTO requests should be submitted in the HR system at least five business days in advance for planned absences.

2.4 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not roll over. If an illness extends beyond three consecutive days, a physician's note may be required.

2.5 Holidays
FreeTime AI observes 11 federal holidays per year. A current holiday schedule is posted in the company intranet each January.

SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION AND PAYROLL

3.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays via direct deposit. Pay periods run Sunday through Saturday.

3.2 Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager.

3.3 Expense Reimbursement
Approved business expenses are reimbursed within 15 business days of submission. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Submit expense reports through the finance portal.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 4 — PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

4.1 Performance Reviews
All employees participate in a formal performance review twice per year — in June and December. Reviews assess goal achievement, core competencies, and growth areas. See the Performance Review Guidelines document for full details on the review process.

4.2 Goal Setting
At the start of each half-year, employees set 3–5 measurable goals in collaboration with their manager. Goals should align with team and company objectives.

4.3 Learning and Development
FreeTime AI provides an annual learning stipend of $1,500 per employee for courses, conferences, books, or certifications relevant to your role. Submit requests through the HR portal for approval.

SECTION 5 — TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT

5.1 Equipment
Full-time employees receive a company laptop and any peripherals required for their role. Equipment remains company property and must be returned upon separation.

5.2 Acceptable Use
Company devices and systems are for business use. Reasonable personal use is permitted but employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems. Prohibited activities include accessing illegal content, installing unauthorized software, or sharing company credentials.

5.3 Security
Employees must use strong, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication on all company accounts. Report any suspected security incidents to security@getfreetime.ai immediately.

SECTION 6 — SEPARATION

6.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees are asked to provide at least two weeks written notice of resignation. Providing notice helps ensure a smooth transition and is appreciated.

6.2 Final Pay
Final paychecks, including any accrued unused PTO, are issued on the next regular pay date following the last day of work, in accordance with applicable state law.

6.3 Return of Property
All company equipment, access credentials, and materials must be returned on or before the last day of employment.
