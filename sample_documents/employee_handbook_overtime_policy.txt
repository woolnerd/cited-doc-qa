Overtime Policy

Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay when they work more than 40 hours in a single workweek. Overtime is calculated at one and one-half times the employee's regular hourly rate.

Managers must approve overtime before the employee works the additional hours, except in emergencies involving customer commitments, safety issues, or equipment downtime.

Employees should record all hours in the timekeeping system by 10:00 AM each Monday. Payroll reviews exceptions every Tuesday. If an employee believes overtime was missed, they should notify Payroll within five business days.

Weekend work is not automatically overtime unless the employee exceeds 40 hours in the applicable workweek. Paid holidays, PTO, and sick time do not count as hours worked for overtime calculations unless required by state law.
