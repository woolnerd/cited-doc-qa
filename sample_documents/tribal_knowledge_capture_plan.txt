Acme Operations Group — Transition Knowledge Capture Plan
Prepared for new ownership onboarding

The following knowledge should be documented from long-time employees during the first 90 days after acquisition:

Customer-specific preferences:
- Which customers require special packaging, labeling, delivery windows, or invoice formats.
- Which accounts have informal pricing exceptions or approval workflows.

Vendor and supplier knowledge:
- Backup vendors for critical materials.
- Which vendor contacts solve issues fastest.
- Seasonal lead time risks and ordering shortcuts.

Production and operations know-how:
- Machine setup tricks that are not in written SOPs.
- Common quality defects and how experienced operators identify them early.
- Maintenance warning signs before expensive downtime.

People and culture:
- Informal team leaders on each shift.
- Employees at retention risk after the ownership transition.
- Training gaps for newer managers.

Finance and administration:
- Month-end close workarounds.
- Recurring manual adjustments.
- Customer collections history that is not obvious from the accounting system.
