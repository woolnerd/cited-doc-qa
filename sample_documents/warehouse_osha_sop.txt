Acme Operations Group — Warehouse Safety SOP
Section: OSHA Procedures and Incident Reporting

OSHA procedures are documented in the Warehouse Safety SOP binder located beside the shipping office, and in the shared Operations drive under Safety / OSHA.

Required procedures include:

1. Forklift operators must complete certification before operating equipment. Recertification is required every three years or after any reportable incident.
2. Aisles, exits, fire extinguishers, and electrical panels must remain clear at all times.
3. Any injury, near miss, chemical spill, or equipment damage must be reported to the Warehouse Manager before the end of the shift.
4. The Warehouse Manager completes the incident report form and escalates recordable incidents to HR within 24 hours.
5. Monthly safety walks are performed on the first Friday of each month. Findings are tracked in the Safety Action Log.

Long-term corrective actions are reviewed during the weekly operations meeting until closed.
