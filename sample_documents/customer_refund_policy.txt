Acme Operations Group — Customer Refund and Credit Policy
Last reviewed November 2025

Refunds are available when products arrive damaged, materially defective, or materially different from the approved purchase order. Customers must submit refund requests within 30 days of delivery.

For damaged shipments, the customer must provide photos of the packaging and product within five business days. Customer Success may issue either a replacement shipment, account credit, or refund depending on inventory availability and customer preference.

Refunds over $2,500 require approval from the General Manager. Refunds over $10,000 require approval from ownership or the CFO.

Custom orders are generally non-refundable once production has started. Exceptions may be approved when the defect was caused by Acme Operations Group or an approved vendor.

Account credits expire after 12 months unless otherwise approved in writing.
